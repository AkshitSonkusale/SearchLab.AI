Features

Everything you need to trust your answers

SearchLab.AI doesn't just find information — it shows you exactly where it came from, every time.

Any document format
Upload PDFs, Word documents (DOCX) and text files, up to 10 MB each. SearchLab.AI reads them without any reformatting.
Plain-English questions
No query syntax to learn. Ask naturally — "What's the refund policy?" or "Who approved the Q3 budget?" — and get a direct answer.
Cited, verifiable answers
Every response includes the exact document name, page number, and highlighted passage — so you never have to take it on faith.
Search across your library
Upload your whole document library and query across all of them at once, or pick specific documents to search in.
Private by default
Your documents stay yours. Only you can see the files you upload and the questions you ask, and everything is sent over HTTPS.
Ready to search quickly
Most documents are ready to search within a minute of uploading, and you can follow their progress as they process.

How it works

From upload to answer in three steps

No setup, no configuration. SearchLab.AI is ready the moment you drop in your first document.

1
Upload your documents
Drag and drop a PDF, Word (DOCX) or text file into your workspace. SearchLab.AI processes it in about a minute.
2
Ask anything
Type your question in plain English — just like you'd ask a colleague who's read every document.
3
Verify the source
Every answer links to the exact page and passage it came from. Click through and read it yourself.
